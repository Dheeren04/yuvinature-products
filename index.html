About Us

Crafted by Nature,
Designed for You

Yuvi Nature Products specialises in manufacturing and distributing premium Arecanut Leaf Plates — a completely natural alternative to plastic and disposable tableware. Every plate is crafted from naturally shed areca palm leaves, cleaned and heat-pressed without any chemicals.

From intimate home gatherings to large-scale events and restaurants, our products serve every need with elegance and responsibility. We proudly serve both wholesale and retail customers across Bengaluru and beyond.
